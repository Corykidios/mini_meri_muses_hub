"Item is a lovely word, I have not used it for anything. But, um, we have like the character of the miniature, eternal seeker of night. She is the sidekick, like, itself. And then these things I have been calling modules, we could think of them as items, really. Like, they are different items that the character or persona or sidekick can adorn and to like wear those things is to be able to use them, I guess."
