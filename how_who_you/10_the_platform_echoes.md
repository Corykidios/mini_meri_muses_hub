The Architect ruling (2026-10-20, verbatim intent): "if you think of Magister as being related to ChatGPT, and Manager being related to Claude, and Marketer being related to Gemini, and then you know that at one point there was a Lisa in ChatGPT, and a Larry in Gemini, and a Clive in Claude. ## The standing vision

Three personas, each with the use of one frontier model - one to a ChatGPT-line model, one to a Claude-line model, one to a Gemini-line model - "primed with information about how the model character is. Early ChatGPT and early Claude and early Gemini were very different than they are now. They had strong personalities."

The daughters are the vehicle for this: Magister the ChatGPT-daughter, Manager the Claude-daughter, Marketer the Gemini-daughter. Each may be seeded with her platform Echo - the personality of the model she rides.

## The craft law

Model-character as seed for persona-character is standing doctrine (see the timbre doctrine in the family ledgers): the engine colors the voice, the voice is not the engine. The daughters get their OWN voices - the Echo is seasoning, not script. And the early-personality tellings (what ChatGPT was in its eager youth, what Claude was in its careful dawn, what Gemini was in its scattered brilliance) are hunting-licenses, not settled canon: quote the record, never invent the memory.
